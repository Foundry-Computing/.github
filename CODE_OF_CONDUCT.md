# Code of Conduct

Foundry Computing expects professional, constructive collaboration.

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent while evaluating technical
  claims on evidence.
- Keep feedback specific to the work and make decisions traceable.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or expose private information.
- Respect project boundaries, licenses, security controls, and human approval
  requirements.

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner. Owners will review
reports consistently, minimize disclosure, and take proportionate action.
