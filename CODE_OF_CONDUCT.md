# Code of Conduct

Foundry Computing expects professional, constructive collaboration grounded in
traceable decisions and respect for people, systems, and project boundaries.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and evaluate technical claims on evidence.
- Keep feedback specific to the work and make decisions traceable.
- Preserve privacy, licenses, security controls, and approval boundaries.
- Document uncertainty, risks, and incomplete validation honestly.
- Support maintainability, repairability, and preservation of knowledge.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or threats
- Exposure of private information, credentials, or sensitive internal details
- Destructive or unauthorized changes to repositories, permissions, or systems
- Misrepresenting testing, approvals, authorship, or AI involvement

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner. Reports should share
only the information needed for review. Owners should respond consistently,
minimize disclosure, and take proportionate action.
