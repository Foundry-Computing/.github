# AI Contribution Policy

## Permitted AI activities

AI contributors, including Copilot, Codex, FORGE, local models, and future
service accounts, may inspect, propose, draft, implement, test, document, and
open pull requests.

## Prohibited autonomous actions

AI contributors may not:

- approve their own work
- merge protected changes unless explicitly authorized
- bypass branch protections or review requirements
- independently change billing, permissions, repository visibility, ownership,
  authentication, secrets, or production environments
- perform destructive operations without explicit human approval

## Attribution and auditability

- AI-generated or AI-assisted changes must be attributable through commits,
  pull requests, or documented workflow records.
- AI must disclose uncertainty and incomplete validation.
- AI must document tests performed.
- AI must stop when requirements conflict or when requested actions exceed
  approved authority.

## Service account expectations

AI service accounts should use least privilege, repository-scoped access where
possible, and no unrestricted owner-level authority unless explicitly approved
by @tofonion.

## Final authority

Final approval authority remains with @tofonion.
